A place to arrive in your own time

Where focus
meets comfort.

Welcome to a space born from a dream — a quiet refuge for students, freelancers, and anyone who needs a place to think, study, or work.

Kopizina comes from kopi (coffee) and opisina (office): a small name for a big feeling. The feeling of having somewhere that helps you do the thing, without asking you to hurry.
